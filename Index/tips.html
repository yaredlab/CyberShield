Safety Tips

Practical habits and checklists to help you stay safe online every day.

01

Use Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Add a second verification step via SMS or authenticator app so unauthorized users cannot access your account.

02

Check Website Addresses Carefully

Phishing sites imitate official portals. Verify domain spellings before logging in (e.g., look for HTTPS and real domains).

03

Cover Your Webcam When Not in Use

Use a physical camera cover or disconnect external webcams to prevent unauthorized camera surveillance.

04

Back Up Important Files Regularly

Maintain encrypted local or cloud backups to protect your critical documents against ransomware extortion.

Strong Passwords

Use a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid using personal information like birthdays or your name. Never reuse the same password across multiple accounts.

A password manager app can generate and store strong unique passwords for each website so you never have to remember them yourself.

Phishing Awareness

Never click unknown or suspicious links, even if they appear to come from someone you know. Always verify the sender's email address and look for signs of urgency, misspellings, or unusual requests.

Legitimate companies will never ask for your password or full card details through email. When in doubt, go directly to the website instead.

Why Cyber Safety Matters

In today's digital world, cyber safety is just as important as locking your front door before sleeping. The internet is full of useful information, entertainment, and communication, but it also contains dangers like hackers, scammers, viruses, and fake websites.

Cyber safety awareness means learning how to protect your personal information online. Use strong passwords with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols, and avoid suspicious links and messages promising unexpected rewards.

Keeping devices updated is important because updates fix security vulnerabilities. Antivirus software, two-factor authentication, and sensible privacy settings can help keep your accounts safe. Be cautious on public Wi‑Fi networks.

Cyber safety is for everyone — students, teachers, professionals, and family members. Stay alert, think before you click, and follow basic safety rules to enjoy the online world securely.
