Cyber safety

In today's digital world, cyber safety is just as important as locking your house door before sleeping. The internet is full of useful information, entertainment, and communication, but it also contains dangers like hackers, scammers, viruses, and fake websites.

Cyber safety awareness means learning how to protect your personal information online. Use strong passwords with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols, and avoid suspicious links and messages promising unexpected rewards.

Keeping devices updated is important because updates fix security problems. Antivirus software, two-factor authentication, and sensible privacy settings can help keep your accounts safe. Be cautious on public Wi‑Fi networks.

Cyber safety is for everyone — students, teachers, workers, and family members. Stay alert, think before you click, and follow basic safety rules to enjoy the online world securely.
