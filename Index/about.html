About Us

An academic initiative designed to foster proactive cybersecurity literacy and empower safe digital habits.

Internet Programming Assignment

CyberShield is an educational platform conceived and built within the Internet Programming course curriculum. Our team envisioned an accessible, visually compelling hub to educate peers and everyday digital citizens on navigating today's threat landscape with resilience and confidence.

By distilling complex security protocols—such as social engineering countermeasures, malware mitigation, and proactive digital identity hygiene—into approachable, interactive learning modules, we aim to make cyber literacy universally actionable. Course Instructor: Mr. Gulelat Yohannes.
