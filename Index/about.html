Internet Programming Assignment

This Cyber Safety Awareness website was developed as part of an Internet Programming course project. Our team designed it to educate students and everyday internet users about emerging threats in the modern digital world.

The platform is built to be simple, approachable, and informative — covering threats like phishing, malware, hacking, and cyberbullying, along with practical safety tips anyone can follow. Course Instructor: Mr. Gulelat Yohannes.
