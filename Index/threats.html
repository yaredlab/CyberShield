What's Lurking in the Digital World?

Understanding cyber threats is the first step to defending yourself online. Here are the most common dangers facing internet users today — especially students and beginners who may not know what to look for.

Phishing
Social Engineering Attack

Fake emails or messages trick users into revealing personal information, passwords, or financial details. Always verify the sender before clicking any link.

Malware
Harmful Software

Harmful software that damages your device, steals your data, or gives attackers control of your system without your knowledge.

Hacking
Unauthorized Access

Unauthorized access to your accounts, systems, or private information by malicious actors who exploit security weaknesses.

Cyberbullying
Online Harassment

Harassment or intimidation through digital platforms — causing real emotional and mental harm to victims, especially young people.

01

Ransomware

A virus that locks your files and asks for money to unlock them.

02

Data Breaches

When companies get hacked and your personal data is leaked.

03

Public Wi-Fi Attacks

Hackers can spy on your data when you use unsafe public networks.

04

Malware (Viruses)

Cyber safety

In today's digital world, cyber safety is just as important as locking your house door before sleeping. The internet is full of useful information, entertainment, and communication, but it also contains dangers like hackers, scammers, viruses, and fake websites.

Cyber safety awareness means learning how to protect your personal information online. Use strong passwords with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols, and avoid suspicious links and messages promising unexpected rewards.

Keeping devices updated is important because updates fix security problems. Antivirus software, two-factor authentication, and sensible privacy settings can help keep your accounts safe. Be cautious on public Wi‑Fi networks.

Cyber safety is for everyone — students, teachers, workers, and family members. Stay alert, think before you click, and follow basic safety rules to enjoy the online world securely.
