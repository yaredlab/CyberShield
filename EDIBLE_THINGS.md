# 🍎 7 Edible Things (Contributor Fuel Guide)

A curated selection of healthy, brain-powering edible essentials to fuel developers, security researchers, and open-source contributors while coding and defending systems.

---

### 1. 🍎 Apples
* **Category:** Fresh Fruit
* **Nutritional Highlights:** High in dietary fiber (pectin), Vitamin C, and natural sugars (fructose).
* **Why it's great for contributors:** Delivers a sustained, crash-free energy lift during intense coding sessions, along with hydration and crunch.

---

### 2. 🍯 Honey
* **Category:** Natural Sweetener / Superfood
* **Nutritional Highlights:** Natural carbohydrates (glucose & fructose), rich in plant antioxidants and flavonoids.
* **Why it's great for contributors:** Indefinite shelf life; provides an immediate natural energy boost and soothes tired throats during long team meetings or late nights.

---

### 3. 🥑 Avocado
* **Category:** Nutrient-Dense Fruit
* **Nutritional Highlights:** Packed with heart-healthy monounsaturated fats (oleic acid), potassium, and lutein.
* **Why it's great for contributors:** Healthy fats support cognitive performance, memory retention, and steady neurological function.

---

### 4. 🌰 Almonds
* **Category:** Nuts & Seeds
* **Nutritional Highlights:** Rich in plant-based protein, Vitamin E, magnesium, and dietary fiber.
* **Why it's great for contributors:** The ultimate desk-friendly snack—no mess, non-perishable, and magnesium helps manage stress and cognitive fatigue.

---

### 5. 🍫 Dark Chocolate (70%+ Cacao)
* **Category:** Confectionery / Superfood
* **Nutritional Highlights:** Powerful polyphenol antioxidants, theobromine, and minimal refined sugar.
* **Why it's great for contributors:** Stimulates endorphins and mental alertness while enhancing focus without the jittery spike of heavy caffeine.

---

### 6. 🥕 Carrots
* **Category:** Root Vegetable
* **Nutritional Highlights:** Loaded with beta-carotene (provitamin A), Vitamin K1, and potassium.
* **Why it's great for contributors:** Protects eye health and combats digital screen fatigue while offering an ultra-satisfying low-calorie crunch.

---

### 7. 🍞 Sourdough Bread
* **Category:** Grains / Fermented Food
* **Nutritional Highlights:** Naturally fermented complex carbohydrates, prebiotic fiber, and bioavailable minerals.
* **Why it's great for contributors:** Easier on the digestive system than standard white breads, offering long-lasting satiety and stable blood sugar levels.
